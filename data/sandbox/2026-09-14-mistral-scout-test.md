## Overview
This topic explores various techniques for optimizing multi-agent workflows to improve efficiency, reduce bottlenecks, and enhance overall productivity.

2. **Task Prioritization Strategies**: Dynamic vs static prioritization
3. **Resource Allocation**: Optimal distribution of tasks across agents
4. **Communication Patterns**: Effective coordination between agents
5. **Error Recovery**: Handling failures and blocked states gracefully
